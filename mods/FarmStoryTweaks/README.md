# Off the Books: Probably a Farm Story Tweaks

![Off the Books: Probably a Farm Story Tweaks](../../pictures/off-the-books-probably-a-farm-story-tweaks-976x544.png)

The farm machines of [Probably a Farm Story](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3832249311) (the mod that used to be called Agri Expansion), a little harder to come by.

| | |
|---|---|
| Mod id | `rnk.farm-story-tweaks` |
| Version | 3.0.0 |
| Needs | Probably a Farm Story. |

## What you get

- **Before:** Dr. Jackson and the Hypertech Smuggler offer every one of its machines on every visit.
- **Now:** each machine has a chance to show up. 25% while you own none of that kind, 5% once you have one.
- **Unchanged:** research requirements, the Integrated Farm and the perfume manual.

## Options

In the Mods list and in Options, under **Off the Books: Probably a Farm Story Tweaks**. Both chances are sliders from 0 to 100.

## Good to know

- It changes what the two sellers have for sale. Nothing is written to your save.
- Needs Probably a Farm Story.

## Other mods

- **Made for** [Probably a Farm Story](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3832249311). It does nothing without it.
- Want to try it with another mod? Make a copy of your save first, and tell me on GitHub how it went.

The full player's guide is [dist/README.txt](dist/README.txt).

## Install

Subscribe on the [Steam Workshop](https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3846399455) and Steam does the rest. Approve the mod and switch it on in the game's
Mods menu, then restart the game.
